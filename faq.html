Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to your questions

Learn about our services, how projects get started, and what to expect when working with ManjuTech.

Browse questions

Services

What services does ManjuTech provide?

Our services include web development, website design and UI/UX, custom software development, SEO and digital marketing, and AI integration. Share your goals with us and we can discuss which services fit your project.

Can you build a website tailored to my business?

Yes. We can discuss your audience, goals, features, and preferred look, then recommend an approach for a custom website or web application.

Do you work on existing websites or applications?

We can discuss updates, new features, integrations, or modernization for an existing project. Contact us with a brief description of the current system and the changes you need.

Project process

How do I get started?

Send us an enquiry with your project goals, requirements, and any timing or budget details you can share. We will review the information and follow up to discuss the next steps.

What information should I include in my enquiry?

Tell us what you want to build or improve, who it is for, which features matter most, and any target dates. Links to examples or an existing website are helpful too.

How long will my project take?

Timing depends on the scope, features, and feedback process. After learning more about your requirements, we can discuss an estimated schedule for your project.

How much does a project cost?

Pricing depends on the work involved and the requirements. Contact us with your project details so we can understand the scope and discuss an estimate.

Support and communication

Can I request support after my project is delivered?

Support and ongoing maintenance can be discussed as part of your project requirements. Let us know what kind of help you expect so we can clarify the available scope and arrangements.

How can I contact ManjuTech?

Use our contact page or send an email to Sahbablu091@gmail.com. For project requests, the enquiry page helps you share the relevant details.

Still have a question?

If you did not find what you are looking for, tell us what you need and we will be glad to discuss it.
